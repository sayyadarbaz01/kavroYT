# Upload Cadences of Animated "What If" and Animated Story/Toon Channels (2025-2026)

Data collection date: 2026-11-18. Most numbers come from vidIQ public stats pages (snapshot dated 2026-11-18) and OutlierKit channel pages (dated Nov 2026). Third-party tracker numbers are estimates: subscriber counts are rounded by YouTube, and the trackers' Shorts vs long-form splits are labeled by vidIQ itself as "estimates from monthly format proportions". vidIQ's "videos in last 28 days" counts **all uploads (Shorts + long-form combined)**. I could not get a reliable separate Shorts/long-form split from any tracker.

## Q1. Which channels lead the animated "What If" / animated explainer-story niche, and what are their numbers?

### Takeaway
The big established players are Zack D. Films (~29.8M, Shorts-first 3D), Kurzgesagt (~25.8M, ~monthly premium long-form), The Infographics Show (~15.5M, near-daily), What If by Underknown (~9.0M, roughly daily in recent weeks) and AumSum (~4.8M). Life Noggin (~3.1M) is dormant and losing subs. IFOLOGY exists and is grouped by trackers with newer "what if" story channels, but I could not retrieve its stats.

## Q2. How often does each upload long-form vs Shorts, and has any changed cadence with what result?

### Takeaway
Cadence across leaders ranges from ~5 uploads per day (Zack D. Films, Shorts) to ~2 per week total (Kurzgesagt). The two near-daily long-form-heavy channels (Infographics Show, What If) are now flat or barely growing, while the slowest (Kurzgesagt) and the fastest Shorts channel (Zack D. Films) are both growing strongly.

### Cited Findings
- Zack D. Films: 136 uploads in 28 days (~4.9/day) vs a lifetime average of ~2/day (4,238 videos over ~1,990 days); +500K subs in 28 days — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@zackdfilms/)
- Tracker counts show Zack D. Films at ~3,614 videos in July 2026 and ~4,109 by Oct 21, 2026 (~495 uploads in ~100 days, ~5/day) — [creatordb](https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/zackdfilms/); [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@zackdfilms/) (search-summary level estimate)
- Kurzgesagt: 7 uploads/28 days, +100K subs/28 days, 400 videos in total — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@kurzgesagt/)
- The Infographics Show: 52 uploads/28 days, 0 net subs — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@theinfographicsshow/)
- What If: 29 uploads/28 days, +10K subs — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@whatifscienceshow/)
- An industry blog argues daily uploading is no longer what triggers the algorithm in 2025 and that consistency matters more than raw frequency (opinion, not data) — [AIR Media-Tech](https://air.io/en/youtube-hacks/the-death-of-daily-uploads-what-cadence-actually-triggers-algorithm-love-in-2025)
- YouTube's own guidance says a consistent, sustainable release schedule is important for building audience expectations — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13616979?hl=en-GB)
- Animated History reached ~100K subs posting only ~0.4 videos/week; OutlierKit says the high production cost and slow schedule make it vulnerable to algorithm shifts — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/channel/animatedhistoryy) (via search snippet)

### Inferences
- In this niche, cadence correlates poorly with current growth for long-form: Kurzgesagt (~2/week incl. Shorts) adds ~100K/month; The Infographics Show (~2/day) adds ~0. Retention and packaging per video seem to matter more than volume for long-form.
- For Shorts-first 3D channels, very high volume does go with strong growth (Zack D. Films ~5/day, +500K/month).
- The Infographics Show "daily vs Kurzgesagt monthly" contrast in the brief holds up directionally in the Nov 2026 data, but I found no source documenting a specific deliberate cadence change at either channel or its effect.

### Gaps
- No primary source (creator interview, Tubefilter piece) on a dated cadence change at Infographics Show, Kurzgesagt or What If was found.
- Per-format upload counts (how many of the 52 Infographics or 29 What If uploads were Shorts) are unknown.

## Q3. What early cadence did 2024-2026 channels that grew fast use (first 20-50 uploads)?

### Takeaway
The only new "what if" channels with retrievable data post ~4-5 long-form videos per week (~10-14 minutes each, no Shorts) in their early phase. Neither is a proven breakout: one is ~1 month old with 3.1K subs, and the other has 60.3K subs but declining momentum. I found no documented first-50-uploads cadence for a verified fast-growing 2024-2026 animated "what if" channel.

### Cited Findings
- What If Levels: ~0.1 years old, 41 videos, ~4.8 videos/week (~20.5/month), ~14:03 average length, ~100% long-form, 3.1K subs, 526K lifetime views (~12.8K views/video, ~19.7K views/day) — [OutlierKit, upd. 2026-11-08](https://outlierkit.com/channel/whatiflevels)
- Ifverse: 32 videos, ~4.4 videos/week (~18.9/month), ~10:28 average length, ~100% long-form, 60.3K subs, ~5.4K views/day lifetime, recent momentum 0.06x (a drop). The channel is listed as ~4.8 years old, so it may be an older channel repurposed to this format — [OutlierKit, upd. 2026-11-07](https://outlierkit.com/channel/ifversetales)
- A vidIQ write-up profiles a channel ("Countdown") that gained 110,740 subs in three months on short videos (not confirmed as animated; cadence not given) — [vidIQ blog](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/100k-youtube-subscribers-new-channel/)
- OutlierKit's growth "case studies" (e.g., raising cadence to 5/week during 10K-100K, or 3/week consistency for a gaming channel) are composite/illustrative, not audited real channels — **anecdotal, do not treat as data** — [OutlierKit gaming case study](https://outlierkit.com/resources/gaming-channel-growth-case-study)
- A faceless-channel guide estimates most faceless channels see consistent growth after 50-100 videos over 6-12 months (marketing guide, anecdotal) — [OutlierKit faceless growth strategy](https://outlierkit.com/resources/faceless-youtube-growth-strategy/)
- Zack D. Films' early phase (2021-2022) reached 500K subs by April 2022 and 1M by August 2022, about 10-14 months after its June 2021 creation; lifetime average ~2 uploads/day — [Social Blade achievements](https://socialblade.com/youtube/handle/zackdfilms/achievements) (search-summary level); [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@zackdfilms/)

### Inferences
- The current pattern among new faceless "what if" story channels (IFOLOGY peers) appears to be ~4-5 long-form uploads/week at 10-14 minutes, with little or no Shorts. Most of these likely rely on AI-assisted or simplified visuals to sustain that pace (OutlierKit describes both as stock/AI/minimalist visuals). Their 20-50-upload results vary widely (3.1K vs 60.3K subs), so cadence alone does not predict breakout.
- A hand-animated 3D toon channel probably cannot match 4-5 long-form/week; the realistic comparison set for such a channel is weekly long-form plus frequent Shorts (inference).

### Gaps
- No verified data on IFOLOGY's start date, early upload count or early cadence.
- No creator interview or Tubefilter profile of a 2024-2026 animated "what if" breakout was found.
- No Social Blade daily history could be retrieved to reconstruct first-3-6-month cadence for any channel.

## Q4. Do Shorts-heavy 3D toon channels (Zack D. Films style) post daily or multiple times per day?

### Takeaway
Yes. Zack D. Films, the benchmark 3D-animated Shorts channel, currently posts about 5 videos per day (136 in 28 days) and has averaged ~2/day over its life, while gaining ~500K subscribers a month at ~29.8M.

### Cited Findings
- 136 uploads in last 28 days (~4.9/day); lifetime ~2/day; +500K subs/28 days; +100K subs/7 days; ~97.5M views/day over the last 28 days — [vidIQ, 2026-11-18](https://vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@zackdfilms/)
- OutlierKit notes the "high production bottleneck and cost" of custom 3D animation makes rapid scaling/daily uploads difficult, which conflicts with the observed upload counts (it may reflect templated/reused assets or a team, unverified) — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/channel/zackdfilms) (via search summary)
- OutlierKit lists Zack D. Films' similar channels as Sambucha, Law By Mike, Visual Venture, Serious History, BE AMAZED, Infinite Universe, Chilling Scares, Professor Historian and The Infographics Show — [OutlierKit](https://outlierkit.com/channel/zackdfilms)
- DaFuq!?Boom! (3D animated shorts, ~43M subs per listicle) grew mainly from one viral animated series — [revid.ai](https://www.revid.ai/blog/best-faceless-youtube-channels) (unverified listicle)

### Inferences
- Shorts-first 3D channels can sustain multiple posts per day, likely through reusable rigs/environments and short (sub-60s) runtimes. Zack D. Films Shorts are generally under a minute (my background knowledge; the tracker length figure of 9:21 is unreliable).
- For a small 3D toon channel, the transferable lesson is that Shorts volume drives subscriber growth much more than long-form volume does in this niche.

### Gaps
- Zack D. Films' team size, production pipeline and exact Shorts length were not confirmed from a primary source.
- No other 3D toon "what if" Shorts channel with verified cadence was found (DaFuq!?Boom! cadence not retrieved).
