## 4. Funnel diagnosis

1. **Impressions → BROKEN.** About 25 per video after 1–2 days, where the target is 1,000+ after 7 days. This is a classic cold start: YouTube has no audience data, so it shows the videos to tiny test groups. **Stop here; this is the main problem.**
2. CTR → looks fine, but the sample is too small to judge.
3. Hook / retention → looks fine, but the sample is too small to judge.
4. Subs → 1. Expected at this stage, and made worse by having no series or playlist to binge.

**Two things that make the cold start worse:**
- **The A/B tests split 7–25 impressions across 2–3 variants.** No variant can ever win at this size, and the test can slow down the early learning signal. Test & Compare needs thousands of impressions to pick a winner.
- **Most views come from the channel page.** If those are your own or friends' views, they teach YouTube nothing about who the real audience is. Short, non-target views can even confuse it.

## 5. Top 5 fixes (priority order)

### 1. Upload more long-forms, on a fixed rhythm, in ONE lane for the next 2 weeks
- **Action:** 3 long-forms a week (for example Mon / Wed / Sat at the same time). Make the next 4–6 videos the *same kind*, ideally the "What If [power/curse] ___ Every Day/Hour" story format that you saw breaking out in Studio Research. The Luck video already fits it, and it got Browse traffic (4 of its 6 views) while GTA got none.
- **Why:** each upload is a fresh test batch of impressions. Repeating one format teaches YouTube one audience faster.
- **Measure:** impressions per video in the first 7 days (target: 200 → 500 → 1,000+, rising with each upload).

### 2. End the A/B tests on the 2 existing videos, and don't start new ones until a video passes ~1,000 impressions
- **Action:** in Studio → each video → Test & Compare → stop the test and keep your favorite title/thumbnail.
- **Why:** the tests can't reach a result at 7–25 impressions. They only split the signal.
- **Measure:** no test running; you can test again once a video has 1,000+ impressions.

### 3. Build the binge path: series playlists + end screens
- **Action:** make playlists **"Kip Tries"**, **"Kip Survives"** and **"Kip's Watch"** (from CHANNEL_PLAN.md), plus one "All What If Stories" playlist. Add an end screen to every video: the best next video + subscribe. Add a "Part of the Kip Tries series" line in the first lines of each description.
- **Why:** suggested traffic (your video showing up next to your other videos) is the cheapest traffic a small channel can get, and playlists also make the channel page look like a show.
- **Measure:** "Suggested videos" and "Playlists" start to appear in traffic sources; views per viewer go above 1.0.

### 4. Fix the Shorts funnel: one Short per long-form, 3–5 a week, hook in the first second
- **Action:** cut 2–3 Shorts from each new long-form (the craziest 20–40 s moment, with a question at the end). In the Shorts editor, set the **"Related video"** link to the matching long-form; it is a tappable link that works far better than a link in the description. Point the Luck video's Shorts at the Luck video, not at GTA.
- **Why:** Shorts get tested on their own feed, and that test is free reach. A 0-view Short after 15 h usually means the first frame didn't stop the scroll, or the feed hasn't picked it up yet. More Shorts = more tests.
- **Measure:** Shorts "viewed vs swiped away" above 60%; Shorts feed views per Short above 500 in the first 48 h.

### 5. Finish the channel page (15 minutes)
- **Action:** add a schedule line to the banner or description ("New Kip What-If every Mon/Wed/Sat"). Set the Luck video as the trailer for non-subscribers. Add the series playlists as sections on the Home tab. Optionally link IFSIM FILMS.
- **Why:** turns the few visitors you get into subscribers ("this is a show, and there's more coming").
- **Measure:** subscriber conversion from the channel page.

**Don't do these right now:** buying promotion, sub-for-sub groups, mass-sharing links in WhatsApp groups. They bring viewers who leave fast, and that teaches YouTube the wrong audience.
