## 4. Will it go viral today? Honest answer
- **Probably not on day 1.** A brand-new channel with 0 subscribers has no audience for YouTube to test the video on. First videos usually get a few hundred to a few thousand views in the first days. They grow later if click-through rate (CTR) and watch time are good.
- **The topic gives it a real chance later.** Demand is proven (millions of views), and our hook and story are stronger than most small competitors'.
- **Shorts are the fastest path.** Shorts on this topic got **1.8M–24.9M views**. Three Shorts cut from this video can bring viewers to the long video.
