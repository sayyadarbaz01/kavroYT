## Spine
1. **Question:** what happens when your luck keeps doubling and the watch won't come off, and where is all that luck coming from?
2. **Escalation:** coin flips and free fries → the Powerball and a half-court shot → the world bends (bus, SUV, casinos).
3. **Twist (planted in Rule 3):** the backward tick means the watch *moves* luck from whoever's closest, and every win was taken from Dev.
4. **Ending:** Kip winds it backward, gives everything back, then loses a coin flip and calls it the best thing all week. KEEP or GIVE IT BACK?
