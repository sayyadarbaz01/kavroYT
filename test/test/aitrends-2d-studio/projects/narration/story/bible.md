# What If You Could Teleport? — story bible

**Logline:** You wake up able to teleport by thought. It starts as wonder, becomes a joyride and then fame, and ends in molecular dread on a sunset park bench.
**Tone:** wonder → playful → glamour → tension → horror → quiet reflection. Second person: "you" is the hero.

## Motifs
- **Teleport:** cyan core `#6fe6ff` + violet edge `#b48cff` particle burst, light column, rings. Rim turns cyan at the moment of a jump.
- **Glitch:** RGB slice displacement. Absent in act 1, faint in act 3, heavy in acts 4–5.
- **Blueprint atoms:** a stick figure made of atoms that disassembles, reassembles, cracks and scrambles as the story darkens.
- **Red** `#ff5a6a` marks danger: misplaced atoms, warnings, failed jumps.
- Freeze-frames at 9:15 (UNSTABLE) and 9:45 (DISASSEMBLY).
