## Spine
1. **Question:** Can a 16-year-old keep a town of little kids alive with zero adults, and what happens to *him* when he turns 18?
2. **Escalation:** best day ever (a staircase of wins) -> water dies, then the lights -> the Kings steal the food -> Kip's own rule nearly freezes Theo -> Marcus turns 18 and is gone by morning.
3. **Twist:** the night he turns 18, everyone waits to see if he vanishes too. The lights die at 11:59; Brody, the boy Kip locked out, restarts the generator. 12:01, he's still here. There's an adult on Earth again, and it's him.
4. **Ending:** payback list, Mom's sticky note, one real-world line (FEMA water rule), and the question: would you have chained that door?

Big loop (0:45 -> twist): "Your eighteenth birthday is two years away, and nobody can tell you what happens when you get there."
Mid-video promise (~4:15): Theo asks if Kip will disappear at 18; Mia paints "730" on the gym wall.
Doubt beat (~5:50): Marcus turns 18; his sleeping bag is empty and his bike is gone. Vanished or ran? Paid off in the ending.

## Cast (one trait each, every trait pays off)
- **Kip** (hero, 16): white face, black hoodie, black cap with a white K, red sneakers, brass watch. Carries Mom's sticky note.
- **Marcus** (Brody's best friend, the only 17-year-old): turns 18 in March and his sleeping bag is empty the next morning. Nobody knows if he vanished or ran. Rides back two weeks after Kip's birthday.
- **Rex** (dog): named in Mom's note; fat and happy at the end.
- **Theo** (8): dinosaur pajamas, asks "Are you in charge now?" Sneaks food to the Kings, gets locked out by Kip's rule, asks again at 12:01.
- **Mia** (16): the only person who liked AP Physics. Map, solar panels, paints the countdown.
- **Lily** (6): pink inhaler, almost empty. Kip pockets the first inhaler for her; she falls asleep on his shoulder at midnight.
- **Brody** (16, varsity quarterback, leader of the Kings): "Kings don't share." Steals the food, carries a freezing Theo to the door, fixes engines, saves the midnight lights.
