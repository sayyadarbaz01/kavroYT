# What If GTA 6 Rules Were Real Life? (full video)

**Format:** 16:9 long video, target 7:00 · Kip (white face, black hoodie, black K cap, red sneakers, brass watch) · narrator speaks to "you", Kip is "you" on screen
**Thumbnail text:** `NO RESPAWNS` · Original Kip animation only: no game footage, logo or music.

## Spine (5 lines)
1. **Situation:** you wake up in Miami and your life runs on GTA rules.
2. **Desire:** use the rules to live with no limits, and later to pay off the bills.
3. **Conflict:** real life fights back with real bills, real cops, real bones. A streamer pays you to hit five stars live.
4. **Change:** you hide three days to lose the stars, come home, and your mom doesn't know you. Rule three was never just about cops.
5. **Result:** you choose to be remembered over being free, and sit across from her until she says your name.

**Big loop (0:32):** "In one week, your own mom won't know who you are." **Twist plant (rule 3, ~2:45):** "Get out of their sight long enough, and they forget you." Viewers assume "they" means the cops. **Hidden clue (~3:00):** Dev texts "hey who is this lol".

## Fact check
- GTA V health regenerates on its own, but only up to half.
- GTA V hospital fee is capped at $5,000 ([gta.wiki](https://gta.wiki/w/Hospitals)).
- The brain starts dying after about 5 minutes without oxygen.
- A broken bone takes 6-8 weeks to heal.
- Average US hospital stay: $10,400 (AHRQ HCUP, 2012).
- Ebbinghaus forgetting curve (1885): most new information is forgotten within a day without review.
- Many US cities use automatic license plate readers.
