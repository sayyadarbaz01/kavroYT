# What If GTA 6 Rules Were Real Life? (YouTube Short)

**Publish:** Nov 19, 2026 (GTA 6 launch day) · **Format:** vertical 9:16 Short, about 56 s, 146 words at 155 wpm · **scriptcheck:** 100/100
**First-frame text:** `NO RESPAWNS` · **Description:** `#GTA6 #Shorts` · Original Kip animation only: no game footage, logo or music.

## Spine (5 lines)
1. **Situation:** Kip gets a five-star wanted level, in real life.
2. **Desire:** escape the way GTA taught him.
3. **Conflict:** every GTA rule fails. Cops don't forget, the dive breaks bones, there's no free respawn.
4. **Change:** he wakes up in a real hospital with a real bill.
5. **Result:** his face is on the news with five stars. The last line loops back to the first.

## Fact check
- GTA V caps the hospital bill at $5,000 when you're wasted ([gta.wiki](https://gta.wiki/w/Hospitals)).
- Average US hospital stay cost: $10,400 (AHRQ HCUP, 2012). "Over ten grand" is safe.
- 30 mph is about 13.4 m/s, and v²/2g ≈ 30 ft, so it's like falling about three stories.
