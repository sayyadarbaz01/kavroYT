## Finding #1: tags, hashtags and chapters do NOT decide whether a video goes viral
- The **2.59M** video has **0 tags, 0 hashtags, 0 chapters** and a 418-character description.
- **DOOMPERK's 1.1M** video: 0 tags, 0 hashtags.
- 8 of the 14 use **zero tags**.
- These videos are found through the **home page and suggested videos** (browse), not search. YouTube shows a video to people who are likely to click and keep watching, so the real ranking factors are **thumbnail CTR + title + retention**.
- Metadata is a hygiene step: it helps YouTube understand the topic and catches some search traffic. It's worth 5–10% of the result, not 90%.

## Finding #2: the title formula is identical across almost all 14
- **"What If You ___?"**, 23–52 characters, one idea, second person.
- One **number or extreme word**: 10x, 500, 1,000, Infinite, −100°C, Everyone's.
- Some brand the casing: "What **IF**" (IFOLOGY, Jeremy Bearimy) or one CAPS word ("in **PRISON**").
- No clickbait words, no emojis, no "(GONE WRONG)".

## Finding #3: the thumbnail does the selling, and its text ≠ the title
| Title | Thumbnail text |
|---|---|
| Reaction Time 10x Faster | **"TOO SLOW."** (the twist: everyone else is too slow) |
| 500 IQ | **"500 IQ" + "TEST BROKE."** |
| 500 Charisma | **"i just said hi.."** (a funny consequence) |
| Creative Mode in Real Life | **"/gamemode creative"** (gamer language) |
| See Everyone's IQ | **"160 IQ" vs "70 IQ"** over two kids, F vs A+ |
| How Long Everyone Has Left | **"52 YEARS" vs "00:00:05"** |
| Lie Count | **"LIES COUNTER" + "9,317 +1"** |
| Kept Getting Nicer | **"YOU ARE TOO NICE" + "60,418,207 people you've hurt"** |

Rules from these:
1. **1–3 words, huge, top-left or top-centre.**
2. **Add a twist or consequence the title doesn't say.** Never repeat the title.
3. **A number or a vs comparison** (160 vs 70, 52 YEARS vs 5 SECONDS, 9,317 +1).
4. The hero shows **one clear emotion**: smug for power fantasies, sweating or shocked for consequences.
5. High contrast: a dark background with neon/gold accents, or a very saturated daytime scene.
6. The black-silhouette stickman with white eyes is the genre look (Ifverse, ConChaser, Ifinite, DOOMPERK, IFOLOGY). Kip's white face stands out in a feed full of black heads, which is an advantage.

## Finding #4: the description is structured for the viewer, not for keywords
The common order:
1. **Line 1 = the exact title question, or a second-person scene** ("What if you had 500 IQ? Not a genius. Not Einstein. Five hundred." / "You got ten years in prison for a robbery you didn't do.")
2. **2–3 lines of escalating stakes + a "but"** ("Sounds amazing. It's also kind of awful.")
3. **A comment question** (a dilemma, or "Who do you think the villain is?")
4. Chapters (optional; 9 of 14 have them)
5. **Real sources / science notes** (All Answers, Miko, DOOMPERK, IFNation, What if U click, Ifverse): "The premise is made up. The studies and history are real."
6. A channel slogan + subscribe line ("Every story starts with IF." / "Ifverse: where every what if becomes a story.")
7. 3 hashtags (YouTube shows the first 3 above the title)

## Finding #5: the biggest hits are SERIES, and the link to the next part is at the very top
- **Ifverse's 4.75M video** starts its description with: "▶ PART 3 (FINAL) is out: … ▶ PART 2 is out: …". It turned one hit into a 3-part chain.
- **Ifinite's 1.87M video** has a pinned comment: "THE STORY CONTINUES: PART 6 IS OUT … Binge the whole series in order: Part 1 → Part 6".
- **Milo** links the previous part and teases the next one ("Next time: what if you LOSE $1 every millisecond?").
- **IFOLOGY** links its next video ("📺 What IF You Were 100x Stronger?").

Every extra video a viewer watches in one session is a strong signal to YouTube. **When a video hits, make Part 2 within 7 days, then pin and put the link at the top.**

## Finding #6: extra tactics
- **Tag-jacking:** IFOLOGY's tags include competitor phrases ("ifverse", "what if your reaction time was 10x faster") so it shows up next to their hits. Milo's tags include its previous title variations.
- **Interactive hook:** IFNation links a free "What If Card Game" round at the top of the description.
- **Category doesn't matter:** Film & Animation, Education, People & Blogs and Entertainment all appear among the 1M+ videos.
- **Length:** 6–17 minutes; most 9–11.
- **Upload time:** mostly 4 AM–1 PM US Pacific (7 AM–4 PM Eastern) = **5:30 PM–2:30 AM IST**. 7:30 PM IST fits.

## Packaging checklist for every Kip video (in priority order)
1. **Topic** in a proven lane (see the Miko, DOOMPERK and Headlamp reports)
2. **Thumbnail:** 1–3 words that add a twist + a number or comparison + one emotion. Make 3 for Test & Compare.

4. **First 30 seconds** pay off the thumbnail (show the climax first)
5. **Description:** title question → stakes + "but" → dilemma question → chapters → real facts → slogan → 3 hashtags
6. **Pinned comment:** the dilemma question now; later "PART 2 IS OUT → link"
7. **Tags (minor):** exact title, 5–8 close search phrases, 2–3 competitor-adjacent phrases, channel name
8. **End screen** → the next video; **playlist** for the series
9. **If it hits 50K in 3 days → Part 2 within 7 days**, then link it at the top of Part 1's description and in the pinned comment
