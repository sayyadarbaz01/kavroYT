# Top 3 AI-video niches where new channels are passing 1M views

Collected 2026-11-21 · 90 search queries · 3,390 channels seen · 1,157 scanned · 113 shortlisted · 6 audited in depth

## What was asked, and what the data supports

The brief was: AI-video niches going viral, where a channel that started about two weeks ago with at least 5 to 6 videos has reached 1M views.

- **Exactly one channel meets the strict test** (first upload 14 days ago or less, 5+ videos, 1M+ views): Little Dream Village, 7 videos, 1.27M views.
- **Seven channels meet it at 21 days**, and **29 at 30 days**. These counts are before removing channels that turned out not to be AI or not to be new. The niches below are ranked on the 30-day set, because one channel cannot define a niche.
- Join dates and total views for the 11 finalist channels were read from each channel's About panel in Chrome. The rest comes from `yt-dlp`.

### 1. AI drama films

| Channel | Joined | First upload | Uploads | Total views | Subs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Hushfall Drama](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UClwGdlzDZ1tKykFnXl407Pw) | 2026-10-30 | 2026-10-30 | 42 (23 long, 19 shorts) | 13,808,598 | 18.7K |
| [Mama's Truths](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCJfs8cQgzfltwEzVCQCQ3JA) | 2025-07-10 | 2026-10-30 | 6 | 7,378,946 | 28.1K |
| [NYS Movie](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCbxyfY6gNc7t1AfD8bipK-w) | 2026-10-21 | 2026-10-30 | 5 | 6,519,734 | 11.5K |

What the audits found:

- **Format.** Fully voiced AI dialogue films with no narrator. Each is one complete story. Both audited channels disclose AI in their descriptions; neither names a tool.
- **Length is rising.** Hushfall's five newest uploads are all outliers and run a median of 22 minutes, against 9 minutes for its older videos. Mama's Truths runs 62 to 68 minutes.
- **Titles.** Two clauses: a concrete wrong, then a concrete reveal. Six of Hushfall's 8 outliers use this form; 1 of its 8 underperformers does. Example: [CEO Paid a Stranger $100,000 a Month to Marry Her](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4m3u8UMUgDg), 2.8M views.
- **Stories are retellings.** Mama's Truths' two hits (4.15M and 2.35M) followed near-identical premises that had gone viral on other channels 13 to 20 days earlier. A search returned 17 look-alike titles on 17 other channels for one of them.
- **Hit or miss.** Two videos hold 87% of Mama's Truths' views. Hushfall has 8 outliers and 8 underperformers out of 22.

Mama's Truths' account dates from July 2025 but its oldest visible video is 22 days old, so it is a reused or wiped account, not a new one. NYS Movie was not audited and its AI use is unconfirmed. The other two in the count are Indonesian: Tya Agustin (31 uploads, 1.26M, titles say "AI DRAMA") and Ayu Rahayu (9 uploads, 1.31M, unconfirmed). Two other channels in this cluster, Seven Clover and Hero Flick, are dubbed live-action Chinese dramas and were excluded.

### 2. Hindi and Bangla AI village stories

| Channel | Joined | First upload | Uploads | Total views | Subs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [sez style](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCXun1vbc5R3-N0WwA1KguSA) | 2026-10-02 | 2026-11-06 | 15 | 9,965,092 | 14.6K |
| [Kahani Qode 2.0](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCOGIpTg73zV9IvnTQ3K261w) | 2026-09-12 | 2026-10-27 | 20 | 3,294,713 | 4.49K |
| [AI Story Bangla](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UChS0TAE_iopSlmM1kV_HguA) | 2026-10-30 | 2026-11-01 | 14 | 1,593,660 | 2.79K |
| [Little Dream Village](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCukO6he70Qmngh87cM3gJOA) | 2026-11-04 | 2026-11-07 | 7 | 1,272,386 | 1.86K |

What the audits found:

- **Format.** 7 to 19 minutes of multi-character Hindi or Bangla dialogue. A threat appears in the first 30 seconds, the sisters pledge an impossible build or capture, a long process middle follows, and the payoff is held to the last 10%.
- **Cadence.** sez style published 15 videos in 15 days. This is the fastest route found: 10M views in 15 days from a standing start.
- **First of a theme wins.** On sez style the first video of each of four themes beat every follow-up. Its top video, [a ghost-scare story](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g8p6-ZDa9QU), has 4.8M views and holds 47% of the channel.
- **The recipe is narrow.** Little Dream Village's five "4 sisters, giant animal, biryani" videos hold all of its views. Its two horror uploads got 127 and 99.
- **It is a copy of a copy.** Little Dream Village tags an older channel, Behno Ka Pyar, and released the same animals at the same runtimes. That older channel has already decayed: recent uploads get 722 to 22,000 views.
- **Weak audience.** Like rates are 0.15% to 0.29%. About half of sez style's comments are other creators asking how it is made. One commenter claims the channel was demonetized; this is unverified.

What the audit found:

- **Format.** 19 to 33 minute Hindi cat-song compilations. Piku Rhymes' description states the music and animation are AI-made and tags Suno and Animon.
- **The burst does not repeat.** Six launch-batch videos hold 99% of Piku Rhymes' views. Its five uploads since about 13 November have 3,600 to 6,800 views each, with the same template.
- **One video can be the whole channel.** Little bit kids' 7.6M comes almost entirely from a single 2.5-minute rhyme.
- **One "new" channel was not new.** Rosoo Tiny TV (4.3M views, 18 videos) is a satellite of an established network with a 3.81M-subscriber flagship. It is excluded from the table.
- Comments are off on these channels, consistent with made-for-kids status, which also limits ad formats.

PikoPlay's AI use is unconfirmed.

## Runners-up

- **AI "rules" horror**: [Lullorin](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCdgIMUacfx6cyAGD8miZesw), 24 videos, 8.35M views, first upload 23 days ago. One channel only, AI use unconfirmed.
- **Historical-figure AI vlogs**: [King Charles II Of Spain](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCMjnDgNr9whL6SgT99-f77w), 7 uploads, 2.6M views, first upload 18 days ago. One channel only.

## What the three have in common

1. **Launch with a batch.** Every audited winner published 4 to 6 videos in its first days. None grew from one video a week.
2. **Views are concentrated.** One to three videos hold most of each channel's total. Plan for several attempts, not one.
3. **The burst decays.** Piku Rhymes, Little Dream Village and Behno Ka Pyar all fell to a few thousand views or fewer per upload within two to three weeks.
4. **Subscribers do not follow.** Subscribers are 0.1% to 0.4% of views on every channel. These views come from recommendations, not a returning audience.
5. **Everything is copied quickly.** Each hit had look-alikes on many channels within days, and one was re-uploaded whole by another channel a day later.

## Recommendation

AI drama films are the strongest of the three. It has the most new channels over 1M, the highest views per video, an adult audience, and comments that show viewers choosing the content knowingly. Hushfall Drama is the only audited channel whose newest uploads beat its older ones.

Village stories are the fastest to 1M but the least durable, and carry the clearest monetization risk. Kids songs produced the largest single burst but no repeat.

## Method and limits

- **Discovery.** 90 AI-video search queries, each run for the last week and the last month, sorted by views. Channels whose result videos summed to 300,000 views or more were scanned: 1,157 of 3,390.
- **Filter.** Whole channel visible in under 40 long videos and under 40 shorts, 5 or more uploads, 1M or more summed views, oldest listed video under 45 days. The exact date of the oldest upload was then fetched.
- **Sample, not census.** A channel that did not appear in those 90 queries, or whose result videos summed to under 300,000 views, was never scanned. Non-English niches outside Hindi, Bangla, Indonesian and Korean are likely under-represented.
- **"Started" means first visible upload.** A channel can delete old videos or reuse an old account, as Mama's Truths shows.
- **"AI" is by search term and disclosure.** It was confirmed from descriptions on the audited channels and is marked unconfirmed elsewhere.
- **Not available from public data:** click-through rate, retention, traffic sources, revenue, monetization status. Any statement here about why a video spread is inference.
- Views are a snapshot from 2026-11-21 and move daily.
