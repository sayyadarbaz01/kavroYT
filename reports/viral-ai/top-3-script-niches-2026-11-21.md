# Top 3 script-driven niches for videos made with Claude

Collected 2026-11-21 · 90 search queries · 4,112 channels seen · 1,041 scanned · 126 shortlisted

This run covers narrated, script-first formats where Claude writes the script, titles and descriptions and another tool makes the voice and visuals. The earlier report covered AI-generated dialogue video: [top-3-ai-video-niches-2026-11-21.md](top-3-ai-video-niches-2026-11-21.md).

The test is the same: first visible upload 30 days ago or less, 5 or more uploads, 1M or more total views. Sixty channels pass at 30 days, 21 at 21 days and 7 at 14 days, before removing gaming and re-upload channels.

## The three niches

| Rank | Niche | New channels over 1M (30 days or less) | Combined views | Youngest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "What if" hypotheticals | 13 | 43.0M | 14 days |
| 2 | History, war and geopolitics explainers | 10 | 26.0M | 20 days |
| 3 | Zombie and survival POV stories | 5 | 11.4M | 7 days |

A further 10 "what if" channels and 7 zombie or survival channels pass at 31 to 45 days, including Nobody Gets In (28 long videos, 12.0M views, 39 days).

- **Format.** Second-person, present-tense survival stories told in days or parts ("30 Days Locked Inside a Supermarket"). IfYou Films' top video is 3,473 words in 24 minutes.
- **Shorts feed the long video.** TRZ_FTB posted "Lockdown" as six shorts, let comments choose each decision, then joined them into one 8-minute video with 711,000 views. WhatWouldYouDo does the same.
- **Hook.** A physical detail that something is wrong: "Your heart beats nine times a minute. And you feel amazing."
- **Local-language openings.** Two of the five are Indonesian (Jam Malam, Ruangcerita21). MELEK, also Indonesian, has 3.3M views at 32 days.
- **Risk.** Dialogue-heavy versions need animation, which is the slowest part to produce. IfYou Films' account dates from 2024-08-29; only its uploads are new.

## Fitness

No AI fitness, workout, transformation or health channel passed the test. Five queries covered it ("ai fitness", "fitness transformation", "workout motivation", "30 day challenge body", "health tips"). The nearest match is body-themed "what if": Benchmark Data's "What Happens If You Never Brush Your Teeth Again?" has 607,000 views.

## Recommendation

Start with "what if". It has the most new channels over 1M, the shortest scripts, the simplest visuals, and the script is most of the product, which is the part Claude does. Its weakness is crowding, so pick a sub-angle the 23 existing channels are not using.

Zombie and survival POV is the better choice outside English: three Indonesian channels passed and no Hindi one appeared in this sample.

History is the only one of the three where a factual error can sink a video, so it needs a source check on every script.

## Method and limits

- Same method as the earlier report: each query run for the last week and last month sorted by views; channels with 300,000 or more views across results were scanned; exact first-upload dates fetched for the shortlist.
- Join dates and total views for nine channels were read from the About panel in Chrome. Where checked, the Views column uses that figure.
- No channel in this run was audited in depth. Format notes come from the top video's transcript and description on seven channels.
- Whether each channel uses AI for voice or visuals was not verified. No visuals were viewed.
- This is a sample. Channels outside the 90 queries were not seen. Views are a snapshot from 2026-11-21.
- Click-through rate, retention, revenue and monetization status are not public.
