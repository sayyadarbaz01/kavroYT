# Launch plans: three AI-video niches for a new channel

Written 2026-11-21. Evidence: the six audits in `reports/viral-ai/` (all dated 2026-11-21) and the raw JSON in `data/viral-ai/<slug>/`. No new data was collected.

## The plan in ten lines

1. Three niches produced 1M+ total views on channels under 30 days old. A plan for each is below; pick one and run it on its own channel.
2. Niche 1, English AI drama films: publish 20 to 48 minute single-story films with "concrete wrong — concrete reveal" titles. Skip the 6 to 12 minute band.
3. Niche 2, Hindi AI village stories: publish daily, 13 to 16 minutes, one fixed cast of five sisters, a new fear or injustice each day in week one, then follow whichever theme wins.
4. Niche 3, Hindi AI cat-song compilations: publish a batch of six 20 to 32 minute compilations inside four days, then stop and measure for a week.
5. In all three, bank the first-week batch before publishing anything. Every evidence channel got its views from a burst, not from subscribers.
6. In all three, expect a hit-or-miss result. One to three videos held 47% to 99% of views on every channel audited.
7. Disclose AI in the description and set YouTube's altered-content label on anything photoreal. Write metadata per video; never paste one description across uploads.
8. Judge packaging at 48 hours and the niche at 7 days, using the rules in each section. One change at a time.
9. Ranking for a newcomer who wants views and monetization: Niche 1 first, Niche 2 second, Niche 3 third. Reasons are at the end.
10. No view or subscriber forecast is made. The audits show what happened to six channels, not what will happen to a seventh.

## Assumptions

Given in the brief:

- Solo creator. AI video, image and voice tools are available.
- Goal: views and monetization eligibility.
- Capacity: about one long video per day for niches 2 and 3; three to four long films per week for niche 1.
- Launch with a batch of 5 to 6 videos in the first week.

Added by me, because they were not given:

- "Day 1" is the first publish day, after the batch is banked. For niche 1, six films at three to four per week means roughly two weeks of production before Day 1.
- Niche 2 is planned in Hindi, not Bangla. Both evidence channels have Hindi audio. A Bangla version would be a separate channel.
- Titles for niches 2 and 3 are given in Romanised Hindi with an English gloss, as asked. sez style published in Devanagari with an English tail; Piku Rhymes published Romanised. Convert niche 2 titles to Devanagari at upload if you want to match the stronger evidence channel.
- Channel names are not chosen. `[CHANNEL]` is a placeholder.
- Emoji are left out of every title here. The Hindi evidence channels used them on hits and flops alike; Rosoo's three emoji titles all underperformed. Adding one is optional.
- The lead premises are original. I did not search YouTube to check whether a near-identical premise is already circulating; the Mama's Truths audit suggests running that check before production (see Niche 1 recipe).
- Platform rules quoted in the Risks sections are general platform knowledge, not collector data. Check the current YouTube Help pages before launch.

## Rules that apply to all three niches

These are general platform knowledge. Verify before launch.

- **Monetization is a review, not a threshold.** The public Partner Programme thresholds are 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 public watch hours in 12 months, with a lower early-access tier in some countries. All five independent evidence channels are past the subscriber line (1,860 to 28,100), and their view totals make the watch-hour line very likely, though watch hours are not public. None of the audits could confirm that any of them is monetized. Approval depends on a review against the channel monetization policies.
- **Inauthentic content** (the policy formerly called "repetitious content"): mass-produced or template-stamped videos that differ only slightly can make a whole channel ineligible.
- **Reused content**: other people's footage, audio or scripts republished without significant original change is ineligible. Generating your own footage from your own script is the safe side of this line; copying another channel's title, description or shot list is not.
- **Altered or synthetic content label**: YouTube requires creators to disclose realistic-looking content made with generative AI, using the setting in Studio at upload. Clearly unrealistic content such as cartoons is generally exempt. Niches 1 and 2 are photoreal and should be labelled. YouTube says the label alone does not limit reach or monetization.
- **Do not clone real voices or faces.** Two comments on Mama's Truths say a voice sounds like a named actor.

# Niche 1: English AI drama films

Evidence channels: Hushfall Drama (13.6M views, 22 long videos with data plus 19 shorts, 21 days) and Mama's Truths (7.46M views, 6 videos of about 65 minutes, 22 days).

## Positioning

For English-speaking adults who want to see a wronged person get justice and a happy ending, `[CHANNEL]` publishes one complete, fully voiced AI drama film per upload, told tighter than the versions already circulating and openly labelled as AI fiction.

## Recipe

**Length.** Two formats only: a 20 to 25 minute film and a 40 to 48 minute feature. On Hushfall, four of five videos of 15 minutes or more are outliers; seven of eight underperformers run 6:34 to 11:26. A 60+ minute film is the Mama's Truths format but needs a four-day cycle, so hold it until after the first 14 days.

**Form.** Dialogue only. No narrator, no intro, no logo. One self-contained story with new characters each time. A restorative ending: villain exposed, kind bystander rewarded. Mama's Truths' one film with a tragic ending drew "not the ending I wanted".

**Beats for a 45 to 48 minute feature.** Timestamps are from the audits.

| Time | Beat | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:25 | Cold open. The first spoken line is the title's wrong | Hushfall: premise spoken at 0:02 to 0:25 in all five transcripts |
| by 2:10 | The audience learns the secret. No character does | Hushfall 2:10; Mama's Truths 1:08 |
| 4:00 | Public humiliation. The title's event is on screen | Hushfall 4:00; Mama's Truths by 4:30 |
| about 5:30 | A kind low-status helper learns the secret and keeps it | Mama's Truths about 5:30 |
| 8:07 | First small reversal | Hushfall 8:07 |

| 43:40 to end | Epilogue mirrors the opening lines. A physical token from act one returns. Close on a question | Hushfall 43:39; token in five of five Mama's Truths films |

For a 22-minute film, scale it (my scaling, not an audit measurement): premise by 0:25, audience secret by 1:30, humiliation by 2:30, first reversal about 4:00, bigger blow about 9:30, reveal about 15:00, payoffs to 19:30, epilogue to the end.

**Script rules from the comments.** Cut any humiliation beat that repeats an earlier one ("the best version of this story, it cuts out the over done ridicule"). Let the victim push back ("sick of them hitting ppl and the person doesn't defend themselves", 53 likes). Review close-up mouths and teeth (609 likes on a teeth complaint). Check props and names for drift between clips.

**Premise check before production.** Both Mama's Truths breakouts followed a 2M to 5M view hit on the same premise by 13 to 20 days. Before scripting each film, run `python3 .claude/skills/youtube-research/scripts/yt.py search "<premise>" --window month --sort views --limit 20` and prefer premises where two channels have passed 1M in the last 30 days. Then write your own story inside that premise. Do not copy a title, a scene or a description.

**Cadence.** Bank six films. Publish six in week one, then three to four per week. Post one short on days without a film. Do not sequel your own hit by swapping props: Mama's Truths went from 4.15M to 112,569 and from 2.35M to 88,963 doing that.

## What to check

**At 48 hours, per video.** Impressions and click-through rate against the other launch films. Retention at 0:30 and at 3:00. Share of views from Browse and Suggested (Mama's Truths' two hits ran at 84 and 148 views per subscriber, so non-subscriber surfaces are the whole game).

- Impressions at or above the batch median but CTR in the bottom third: swap the title to the next of the top three. Leave the thumbnail. Re-check 48 hours later; if still bottom third, swap to thumbnail B.
- CTR fine but the 0:30 retention is the lowest in the batch: the open is the problem. Fix the next script; leave the packaging.
- Low impressions and normal CTR: leave it. A repackage will not fix a video YouTube is not testing.
- Never change title and thumbnail together, and do not touch a video that is still climbing.

**At 7 days.** Views per day by pillar and by length band. Average view duration as a share of runtime for the 22 against the 45. Subscribers gained per 1,000 views (Mama's Truths: 3.77). Which pillar gets the Day 14 slot. Re-check any low performer at day 14 before dropping its pillar; the audits could not judge videos under a week old.

## Risks

- **Hit or miss.** Hushfall: 8 outliers and 8 underperformers from 22 long videos; three videos hold 59% of long-form views. Mama's Truths: two videos hold 87%. A third of films failing is the normal case.
- **Decay.** Mama's Truths: each upload after the two hits has fewer views than the one before (4.15M down to 88,963). Hushfall slumped to mostly under 55K per video around 6 to 13 November before recovering with longer films.
- **Commodity premise.** One search found 17 look-alike titles on 17 channels for a single premise. Mama's Truths' top film was re-uploaded by another channel within a day. Commenters notice: "This is the 4th version I've seen."
- **Inauthentic and reused content.** Footage and scripts generated for the channel are original. Borrowing premises is common in the niche, but lifting a title, description or scene sequence from another channel moves toward reused content. Template plots at daily volume move toward inauthentic content. Keep a dated script file for each film.
- **Synthetic-content label.** These are photoreal people in realistic settings. Label every upload. Hushfall discloses in the channel description and each video and its comments are positive; five of six Mama's Truths videos carry no disclosure.
- **Voice likeness.** Do not use a voice that imitates a known actor.
- **Seeded engagement.** The Mama's Truths comment sections show signs of templated comments. Do not do this.
- **Production cost.** A 45-minute film with consistent characters is the most expensive unit in this report. Hushfall's 48-minute film still shipped with a wrong seat number in the description and drifting names.
- **Seasonal titles.** Both Thanksgiving titles on Hushfall underperformed (two videos; a lead, not a pattern).

Evidence channels: sez style (10.25M views, 15 videos in 15 days) and Little Dream Village (1.27M views, 7 videos in 14 days).

## Positioning

For Hindi-speaking family viewers who enjoy village spectacle, `[CHANNEL]` publishes a daily 13 to 16 minute, fully voiced AI story in which the same five sisters meet a fear or an injustice and answer it with one impossible build, ending in forgiveness and labelled as AI fiction.

**Two findings that pull against each other, and how this plan handles them.** On sez style the first video of a theme beat its follow-ups in all four themes. On Little Dream Village one repeated recipe carried every view and leaving it dropped views to about 100. So: week one opens six themes, one per day. Week two alternates a new theme with a follow-up to whichever theme led at 48 hours. Never change genre away from "five sisters plus a build" on this channel.

**Title rule.** Open on the fear or the injustice, then the spectacle. For fear stories, hide the answer: the 4.8M title withholds it and the three later ghost titles that give it away sit at 0.73x to 1.20x. That rests on one video, so treat it as a test.

**Honesty rule.** Little Dream Village's titles say biryani "of" the animal, and the animal is never cooked. Do not copy that. Say what is cooked and that the animal is released.

**Edit check.** Watch each export once at speed. sez style has dialogue blocks repeated word for word at 453 s and 463 s. Delete template labels from pasted descriptions.

**Cadence.** Daily, no gaps. Bank six before Day 1.

# Ranking for a newcomer

1. **Niche 1, English AI drama films.** Two independent new channels reached 13.6M and 7.46M. Neither evidence channel is confirmed monetized.
3. **Niche 3, Hindi AI cat songs.** One independent channel is the whole proof, 99% of its views came from one launch batch, and identical uploads two weeks later get about 4,000 views. Made-for-kids rules cut revenue per view and remove the tools for building an audience, and kids content faces the strictest quality review. Run it only as a one-batch experiment with a fixed budget.

If the only goal were the fastest first million views, Niche 2 would move ahead of Niche 1. With monetization eligibility in the goal, the order above holds.

## Limits

- Every number here comes from six audits of public data taken on one day. CTR, retention, traffic sources, revenue and monetization status of the evidence channels are unknown.
- The evidence channels are 14 to 22 days old. Several patterns rest on one or two videos and are marked as tests.
- The lead premises and all working titles are untested proposals. No momentum search was run on them.
- Platform policy statements are general knowledge and may have changed.
