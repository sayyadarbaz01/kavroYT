## Verdict
In 14.1 days the channel posted 15 long-form 2D "What If" stories (one a day), and they have 1,442,675 views in total. 67.5% of those views come from three uploads: #6 Heatwave, #8 College Wife and #10 Secret Training, at 4.48x, 7.24x and 9.41x the median.

The biggest lever is the premise. A forced-proximity or glow-up "for your CRUSH" setup with rules and a day countdown (examples: "What If You Trained in SECRET for 90 Days for Your CRUSH?", 433,030 views; "What If Your College Assigned Your CRUSH as Your Wife?", 333,893) works when it is packaged with a close two-shot thumbnail of Gus and Lia.

The biggest risk is that the thumbnail does much of the clicking. In the top-30 comments, 11 of 30 on Heatwave and 9 of 30 on College Wife are about the thumbnail. Commenters call it suggestive ("how is youtube not eliminating this guy", 54 likes). That is a policy and brand-safety exposure for an audience that includes at least one self-identified 9-year-old.

- **Public data only.** CTR, retention, impressions, traffic sources, revenue and subscriber history are unavailable; the traffic notes above are comment clues only. With YouTube Studio figures, these findings could be checked against real retention and traffic sources.
- **Cumulative views only.** Views are totals at fetch time. "First breakout" ranks uploads by cumulative views and x_median, not by how fast each grew at the time. Lifetime views/day favours the newest uploads.
- **Dates and timestamps.** The channel listing's dates are approximate and its stats use them (7.54 uploads/week). The upload order, IST times and gaps use the exact timestamps from `data/fast-growth/times.txt`, with 3 of 15 re-verified.
- **Unknown channel creation date.** Day 0 is the first upload.
- **No shorts or streams.** Both tabs returned no data (initial run and retry), so there is no shorts baseline.
- **Comments are a sample.** They are the top 30 by YouTube's ranking per video, and fewer were returned on some videos (3 to 30). The collector does not report total comment counts when fetching comments.
- **Judgement calls.** The thumbnail descriptions and the "suggestive" classification are a visual read of the 15 maxres thumbnails. Treating chapters as auto-generated is an inference.
