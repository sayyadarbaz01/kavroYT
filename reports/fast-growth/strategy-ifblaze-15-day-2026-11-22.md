# How 3 new What-If channels hit 1M+ views in 15 days, and the IfBlaze 15-day plan
2026-11-22 · ConChaser, Hypo Room, Gus Maybe · 63 long videos, 12 Shorts · exact publish timestamps

Sources:

- Trend ideas: `reports/fast-growth/trends-30d-whatif-2026-11-22.md` (259 English what-if videos from the last 30 days).

## Summary
1. **Views start at video 1 to 6, not after months.**
   - ConChaser: video 1 got 202k and video 3 (day 2) got 2.41M. Its first 8 videos made 7.08M views in 8 days.
   - Hypo Room: video 1 flopped at 8k; video 2 (day 1) got 1.50M.
   - Gus Maybe: video 1 got 4.8k, video 2 got 41k, and video 6 (day 5) broke out.
   - YouTube shows a new channel's video to viewers of similar channels straight away. A video that gets clicked and watched spreads within 24 to 72 h.
2. **Volume: one long video every day.**
   - 28 to 29 videos in 29 days (ConChaser); 19 in 19 days (Hypo Room); 15 in 14 days (Gus Maybe).
   - Breakout rates: ConChaser 10 of 29 videos at 2x median, Hypo Room 8 of 19, Gus Maybe 3 of 15.
   - Most videos do not break out. Daily uploads buy more tries.
3. **Each channel repeats one premise family.**
   - ConChaser: "What If You Could See Everyone's [stat]?". 17 titles; 10 of its 10 outliers.
   - Gus Maybe: "What If You [trapped / forced / trained] … Your CRUSH?". 12 of 15 titles. The 3 without "crush" all came in below median.
   - Hypo Room: "What If Humans Never Invented X?" and crush-stuck-somewhere stories.
4. **Packaging gets the click. SEO does not.**
   - ConChaser has no tags on 26 of 29 videos, uses category People & Blogs, and has 11.7M views.
   - Hypo Room uses 10 to 18 tags on every video. Both grew the same way, so traffic is browse and suggested, not search.
5. **Thumbnails: same cast, two characters, one clear contrast.**
   - ConChaser: a glowing number with an arrow over each head, green for the hero and red for the rival.
   - Hypo Room and Gus Maybe: suggestive drawings of the female character, and top comments talk mostly about the thumbnail. **IfBlaze will not copy this.** It breaks your rules and puts the channel at risk (a 9-year-old commenter on Gus Maybe).
6. **Scripts.**
   - The premise is shown in the first 0 to 30 s, then a rewind ("let me explain").
   - Rules, a rival, a day counter, a twist at the midpoint, a moral.
   - The video ends on a two-part question to the viewer.
   - 13 to 16 minutes at 145 to 195 wpm.
7. **Fixed daily slot in the US afternoon or evening.**
   - ConChaser: 1:00 to 2:30 AM IST (2:30 to 4:00 PM ET).
   - Gus Maybe: 3:30 to 5:00 AM IST (5:00 to 6:30 PM ET).
   - Hypo Room: 12:30 AM IST or 9:30 PM IST.
8. **Shorts did not cause any of the breakouts.** ConChaser and Gus Maybe have no Shorts. Hypo Room started Shorts after it grew: 12 Shorts have 130k views, against 5.37M for its long videos.
9. **After a hit, a new sibling within 24 to 48 h works; a remake fails.**
   - ConChaser IQ (2.94M) was followed the next day by Future Height (1.46M).
   - Remakes of its own hits came in at 0.16x to 0.99x median: "Imagine If… IQ" 90k, Bank Balance 117k, "Follow Their Own Dating Standards" 23k.

10. **Your 3 existing videos sit in cooling or crowded families** (trends report).
    - "See everyone's X" is cooling: Lookif's rating sequel has 13k views.
    - "Humans never invented X" is saturated: copies of the Clothes video have a median of 7,273 views, and the only mirrors copy has 204.
    - Crush time loop: the best of 4 videos has 37,747 views.
    - The new videos lean on families that are still rising: survival over a set time, waking up in another time, personal power, zombie outbreak.

**Cadence:** one long video a day is what the winners did.
- If the pipeline cannot make one a day, publish every second day. That is 7 to 8 tries instead of 15, so lower odds.

### Every video must have
1. **Title:** the family template, under 60 characters, with one stat or one trap. One capitalised word is allowed (CRUSH, IQ).
2. **Thumbnail:**
   - The same two IfBlaze characters, high-contrast colours, a 2-3 word headline.
   - For the "See" family, a glowing number with an arrow over each head (green for the hero, red for the rival).
   - No suggestive poses or clothing. Score 95+ or remake it.
3. **Hook:**
   - The title's premise on screen within 0 to 20 s, with a contrast (low number on the "winner", high number on the hero).
   - Then "Let me explain."
4. **Body:**
   - The rule, a rival, a day counter, three escalations, a twist at around 50%.
   - 13 to 15 min at about 150 wpm, which is about 2,000 words.
5. **Ending:** a two-part question to the viewer ("Would you want to know yours? And would you still…?"), then point to the next video by name.
6. **Studio:**
   - Description with the title as a question plus 2 lines.
   - Links to 2 other IfBlaze videos and the playlist; chapters.
   - 10-15 tags; category Film & Animation or Entertainment; not made for kids.
   - End screen to the best video.
7. **First hour:** pinned comment with the same two-part question; reply to the first 20 comments.

### Daily check (48 h after each upload)
Open Studio > Analytics > Advanced for the video.

| Signal | Keep going | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Click rate in first 48 h | 6% or higher | below 4%: replace the thumbnail and test with A/B (Test & compare) |
| Average percentage viewed | 40% or higher | below 30%: cut the slow opening, get to the premise faster |
| Views per hour at 24 h | rising | flat after 72 h: stop that family, use the next one |

If a video passes 50k views in 48 h, make 2 siblings of it within 4 days (day 10 and day 13 above).

### What not to copy
- **Suggestive thumbnails** (Hypo Room, Gus Maybe). They drive clicks, but they bring child viewers, limited ads and possible strikes.
- **Third-person "Alex" stories and remakes of your own hits.** ConChaser's newest 10 videos are mostly these, and their median is 83% below its first 10.
- **Unedited auto-descriptions and placeholder links.** These appear on ConChaser video 1 and Gus Maybe Heatwave.

## Realistic odds
- All three channels here were picked because they broke out. That is survivorship: many channels doing the same thing never break out.
- In the earlier 32-channel sample, 9 of 32 new what-if channels had one of their first three videos pass 1M; none had all three pass.
- With daily uploads in the strongest families, 1M total views in 15 days is possible but not promised.
- 1K subscribers usually comes with the first video that passes about 200k views.
- 4,000 watch hours needs about 80k+ public long-form views. Ad views, unlisted views and Shorts don't count.

## Method and limits
Public data from yt-dlp on 2026-11-22. No click rate, retention, impressions or traffic sources exist for other channels. "Browse and suggested drives growth" is inferred from three things: no-tag channels growing as fast as tagged ones, the views coming within days, and comments about scrolling past thumbnails. The thumbnail notes come from the audits.
