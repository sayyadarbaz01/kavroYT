## Headline
The niche is almost entirely new channels. Of the 66 small channels that broke out (under 50k subs, a video over 100k views at 50+ views per subscriber), 65 have their oldest long-form upload marked "1 month ago" or newer, so they are under about 60 days old. The one exception is What If Tomorrow, at about 1 year.

The biggest steady demand is long survival stories:
- [Trapped In Walmart After Closing 1 Year](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3pkkFlH7-pU): 3,894,112 views in 20 days.
- [Zombie Outbreak: What If YOU Were Patient Zero?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6qIJI38aTmE): 1,994,942 views on 8,050 subs.

The hottest theme this week is crush stories where two people are forced together: 26 of its 38 videos are 7 days old or less, with a median of 46,279 views/day. Much of that speed comes from suggestive packaging. The top commenters on [What If Your CRUSH Could Feel Everything You Do?!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RzOKtiNZRUg) talk about almost nothing but the thumbnail.

Three title templates have already crashed under copies:
- "Humans never invented clothes": 30 videos, median 7,273 views.
- "Nerd dated the popular girl": 11 videos, median 530 views.
- "Time loop until you beat Prime X": the median for the last 7 days fell to 608 views/day.

The "see everyone's X" theme is still the largest by views (21.4M), but it is cooling. All three @IfBlaze topics sit in these crowded or cooling families.

## Patterns
**Titles**
- 18 of the top 20 by views/day start with "What If" and use "You".
- 8 of 20 put one word in ALL CAPS (CRUSH, YOU, HOUSE, PRISON).
- 6 of 20 include a number (500, 20,000, 1 Year, 30 Days).

**Length and metadata**
- Median length is 895.5 s for the top 20 and 640 s for all core videos.
- Survival winners are long "(Full Story)" or "(Full Movie)" uploads. 5 of the 7 such videos have over 700k views.
- [IfYou Films](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6qIJI38aTmE) re-cut its [448-second original](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4phfD_oBoY) (813,927 views) into a 1,415-second full story, which got 1,994,942.
- 16 of the top 20 have chapters. 9 of the top 20 have no tags.

**Hooks (from transcripts)**
- [Patient Zero](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6qIJI38aTmE) opens with second-person physical symptoms ("Your heart beats nine times a minute") and states the premise at 22 s. A real fact (the fungus that controls ants) arrives by 40 s, then "Rule one / two / three". The new angle is that you are the cause of the outbreak, not a survivor.
- [Reborn as Your Little Sister's Baby](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GNYhvomRrAs) opens on a sibling fight, transforms the narrator by 16 s, and proves it with a scar detail at about 52 s. The angle is an emotional family twist, not romance.
- [Born With Infinite IQ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_4UaoCZFXsQ) asks its question at 0 s, turns it at 33 s ("Here's the problem"), and ends on "are you happy?".
- [Woke Up 20,000 Years Ago](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=myAGmeMR_vQ) uses a documentary tone and opens on how useless you would be.

**What commenters ask for**
- IfYou Films' creator asked whether viewers want parts 4 and 5 or a season 2 (79 likes).
- Real Aside: "I need a part 2 please". Another commenter is glad it is "not a 'if you breath, you get a girlfriend' video".
- What If Effect: "PLEASE MAKE MORE INFINITE IQ VIDEOS".
- The Hidden Why: commenters raised disease (635 likes) and the language barrier (221 likes). The video does not cover either.

**Sequels decay.** Lookif's Part3 above drew 6.12 views/sub.

**Copy pressure.** Tutorials teaching this format are viral:
- [How to Make Viral Animated Shorts Using Claude](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OtbLL6OBNE4): 1,305,538 views in 21 days.
- [I Made VIRAL 3D Animation Videos Using ONLY Claude AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gu9lO8oqGYU): 207,779 views in 7 days.

Expect copy waves to keep shortening the life of each template.

**Do not copy.** Much of the crush and "only guy" speed comes from suggestive packaging.
- The top comments on [Curious Guy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RzOKtiNZRUg) are about the thumbnail ("Budget went straight to the Thumbnail", 474 likes; "beware of YT rules, you're on the edge").
- Other examples: [ONE BED](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PdLevgvh3qs), [Only Man on an Island of 100 Amazons](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UtXsABU4Cek), [Girls Only School](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SCEGYw2LjG4).
- Many crush videos are set in high school. The ideas below use college students or adults.

## Method and limits
**Queries**, each run in the month window (30 results) and the week window (20 results), sorted by views:
1. "what if animation"
2. "what if you could see everyone's"
3. "what if humans never invented"
4. "what if time loop"
5. "what if your crush"
6. "what if nerd school animated story"
7. "what if survival animated"
8. "what if you woke up"
9. "what if animated story"
10. "what if everyone could"

**Collection**
- 345 unique videos after merging. 74 were dropped as off-topic (Minecraft, Roblox, anime fan fiction, Hindi or Bengali, tutorials, podcasts), and 5 borderline ones were added back.
- I enriched all 276 candidates, not just the top 30, because the brief needed small channels and saturation counts.
- 4 videos were over 30 days old and dropped.
- 9 more were dropped because their native titles are not English: WhatIfVerse, Başına Gelse, Entropi, ifstudioalikh, Zetta Data, Maya Vignanam. YouTube had shown them with translated English titles.
- 4 more were dropped as off-topic: an AI explainer, an ASMR video, a 3D channel with 9M subs, and a 6-hour compilation.
- That leaves 259: 241 core videos and 18 from nearby formats.
- Transcripts and 30 comments each were read for 5 videos.
- Channel-tab lists were pulled for 66 breakout channels.

**Retries and gaps**
- Three searches returned null views (q05-month, q08-week, q10-week). They succeeded on retry.
- Batches containing ids that start with "-" failed until re-run as full URLs.
- 7 videos still have null subscriber counts after a retry, so they have no views/sub: 3 Stupid Dupid collabs, Price of If, IfStorm, Mr.Husey, Jake Mors.
- Collab uploads report wrong subscriber counts on the `video` endpoint, so I used channel-tab counts for 3 of them.
- `channel @IfBlaze` returned no data on both tries.

**Limits**
- Channel age comes only from the videos tab, as rounded labels. Shorts, deleted videos and the real creation date are not visible.
- Search results depend on region and ranking, so this is a sample, not a census.
- Views are a snapshot from 2026-11-22.
- Views/day for 1- to 3-day-old videos is front-loaded, which inflates the 7-days-or-less medians of some themes.
- No CTR, retention or impression data.
- "Suggestive" flags are my judgement from titles, descriptions and comments.
