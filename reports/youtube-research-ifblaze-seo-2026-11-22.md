# YouTube SEO research: If Blaze launch videos
2026-11-22 · 9 search queries · 135 search results · 26 videos checked in detail · raw data in `data/seo-launch/`

## Summary
1. **Tags do not drive views in this niche.** The four biggest videos checked have 0 or 1 tags: [See Everyone's IQ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2TsZSQ77E9o) 2.92M (0 tags), [How Long Everyone Has Left](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u2GyTKhHsTQ) 1.89M (0 tags), [Lie Count](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MGZdDZyQUGg) 961k (1 tag), [Rates You 1 to 10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lu9p69RkagA) 597k (0 tags). A copy with 30 tags, [RealityX](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=urghmOc9dFE), has 95 views.
2. **Search rank #1 is reachable for videos 1 and 2, but it will not bring 1M views.** YouTube autocomplete has no suggestion for "see everyone's rank" or "time loop with your crush", so few people type them. The 1M videos get their views from the home page and suggested videos. That is decided by thumbnail, title and how long people watch.
3. **All three ideas already have copies.** "Rank Out of 8 Billion": 7 videos, best 244 views. Mirrors: at least 8 what-if videos, best 770 views ([If Not.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R17jCzFmF9I), 8,000 subs). Crush + time loop: 11 videos since Nov 10, best 37k ([Jeremy Bearimy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V0t_OmCIZLc), 64.8k subs).
4. **Small channels can still break out.** [Lookif "Real-Life Rating"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kS222TGe8Nc): 338k views with 2,120 subs. [Possiblo "Stuck in a Car With Your Crush"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W0XEzHIHOlw): 363k in 6 days with 3,650 subs. [Nero time loop](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WeM0h6NCoQ4): 432k with 3,790 subs.
5. **The winning thumbnail format is fixed.** Dark scene, neon colour, two big faces, a glowing number over each head that contradicts the other, and 2 or 3 words of text. See `data/seo-launch/thumbs/sheet.jpg`.
6. **Chapters are common among winners.** 4 of the 6 biggest videos checked have 9 to 25 chapters. Description length varies from 74 to 2,319 characters, so length itself does not matter.
7. **Mirrors has the weakest demand.** No what-if mirrors video is above 770 views. The only large one is a 2-minute comedy sketch from a 907k-sub channel (4.46M).

## What to do
- **Title:** keep the exact search phrase at the start. Packs give one lead and one A/B title per video.
- **Thumbnail:** make custom neon art in the format above. Do not use a raw frame; the video frames are pale.
- **Description:** exact title phrase in the first sentence, a comment question, links to the other two videos, real chapter times.
- **Tags:** 11 to 13 tags, exact title first. Low effect; done for completeness.
- **Captions:** upload the `.srt` file so YouTube indexes the exact words.
- **After upload:** use Studio "Test & compare" for thumbnails. Change the thumbnail first if impressions are fine but click rate is low.

## Method and limits
Collected 2026-11-22 with yt-dlp (public data) and YouTube autocomplete. Search results change by region and by day. No click rate, retention or impression data exists for these videos or for If Blaze. @IfBlaze returned no uploads, so there is no channel history to judge from. 1M views cannot be promised: in the earlier sample, 9 of 32 new what-if channels had one of their first three videos pass 1M and 0 of 32 had all three.
