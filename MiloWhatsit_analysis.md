## 2. The winning formula: "What If You Earned $1 Every [tiny unit of time]?"

### Why it works
1. **One number, instantly understood:** "$1 / second." No setup needed; even a 10-year-old gets it.
2. **Escalating money milestones by age** (the retention engine):
   - Part 1: Age 1 = 30 tons of cash → age 31 billionaire in Times Square
   - Part 2: 16 min old = millionaire → day 11 = billionaire → age 31 = **trillionaire**
   Every ~30 seconds there's a new, bigger number. Viewers stay to see the next one.
3. **Emotional anchor that money can't buy:** a $2 string bracelet from a friend (Sam), Grandpa's slow walk to the ice cream shop.
4. **Recurring villain + a deal:** "the man in the gray suit" offers an upgrade or contract → **the choice** at the end.
5. **Comment bait:** "Would YOU rip up that contract?" → 212 comments.
6. **Sequel escalation:** Part 2 is "1,000× faster than last time" and got **2× the views of Part 1**. Each description links the previous part and teases the next ("Next time: what if you LOSE $1 every millisecond?").

### Why Part 3 ($1 Every Step) is weaker so far
- A step is *slower* than a second → the numbers feel smaller. The series escalation went backwards.
- Thumbnail sub-label says "1M ROBUX A DAY", which doesn't match the title (confusing).
- 9:37 long vs 6:00 for the hits: a simple money premise doesn't need 10 min.

### Why the others flopped (4–12K)
- **Tiny / 10 Super Powers:** generic what-if topics with hundreds of existing versions; listicle science with no emotional story.
- **North Korea:** heavy real-world topic tied to a game trend (Call of Duty); 11-minute documentary feel.
- Titles mix "WHAT IF / What IF" casing → looks less polished.

## 3. Thumbnail rules (from the 2 hits)
- **Super minimal:** mascot on the left with a shocked "o" mouth, **huge "$1/SECOND" text** on the right, a tiny red sub-label ("$86,400 A DAY", "1,000× FASTER").
- Cash and coins flying, warm orange → purple gradient.
- Only one idea per thumbnail. The number is the hero.
- Flops use busy, cluttered scenes (atoms, North Korea crowd, giant cookie).
