## 3. Thumbnail system
- **Hero is SMUG and confident**, not shocked (power fantasy). Miko's hero is shocked; DOOMPERK's is cool.
- **Giant yellow symbol/number:** "∞ IQ", "∞$ 24H", "2 SEC LAG", "ADMIN", "3/10 → 10/10".
- **Before → after comparison:** 100 IQ vs ∞ IQ, 2000 ms vs 0 ms, push-up 1 → 15, EF0 → EF5, Day 1 → Day 30.
- **Authority figures** shown small and shocked behind the hero (guards, a drill sergeant, a captain).
- Bright daytime colors, high saturation, teal hoodie = brand color.
- Rating/romance thumbnails: girls with floating tags ("FRIEND", "FUTURE WIFE 99%", "3/10, 10/10").

## 4. Story / script system
- **Opening line drops you mid-action:** "Day one of basic training. A drill sergeant is screaming one inch from your face…", "You got ten years for a robbery you didn't do. Six hours in, you've already found three ways out."
- **Chapters named like game levels:** "Level 0: The Case", "Level 1: Tank"…
- **Named side characters** (Ryker the football captain, Drill Sergeant Bass, Raven Blackwood), which makes it feel like a movie.
- **Escalation ladder:** small win → bigger win → whole system → national-scale stakes ("at 3:07 a.m., every warning screen in America lights up").
- **The catch** (brand promise: "every perk has a catch") + a moral choice ending.
- **One-word comment vote:** "Tell us RYKER or ROCKS." Easy comments.
- **Length grew over time:** 6–9 min in October → **13–16 min** for the November hits.

## 5. Cadence and momentum
- **Daily uploads**, a 74% 100K+ rate in the first two weeks, then a mix of misses and two 1M hits.
- Recent misses (Domain Expansion, Saitama, Infinite Money, Won Every Argument) show that **even big channels flop when they leave their winning lanes.**
