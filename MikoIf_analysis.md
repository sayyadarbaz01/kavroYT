## 2. THE winning formula (why 10K+ happens)

### A. Topic: "What If You Could SEE ___ above everyone's head?"
8 of 19 videos use this exact premise. It's the engine of the channel:

| "See ___" topic | Views | Why it worked / failed |
|---|---|---|
| Lie count | 939K | Trust + betrayal + a **number** |
| Crush timer | 630K | Romance, teen-school setting, "who likes me?" |
| Body count | 294K | Taboo/spicy, controversial → 402 comments |
| Time left (death) | 203K | Death + number |
| Who loves you most | 176K | Romance + betrayal (bride loves best man) |
| Real friends | 16.5K | Too soft — no romance, no taboo |
| Hidden talent | 15.4K | Positive/neutral = low tension |
| Regrets | 5.9K | Sad, abstract, nobody "wants" to know |

**Rule:** the hidden thing must be something viewers *secretly want to know about people around them* — **love, lies, sex, death, betrayal**. Soft/introspective things (regret, talent, friendship) flop even with the same thumbnail style.

### B. Second winning lane: famous character trains/changes YOU
- *Goku Trained You for 1 Year* → 251K in **1 day** (fastest on the channel). Big fandom + "Day 1 vs Day 30" transformation.
- *MrBeast Bought Earth* → only 13.8K. Real-person parody with legal disclaimers feels weaker than an anime power fantasy.

### C. Power/curse premises = steady 40–50K floor
"Everything you said came true" (51K), "Falling asleep killed you" (47K), "Erase anything" (43K). Good, not explosive — no "number above heads" visual and no romance hook.

### D. What flops
- Sad/medical-drama topics (Declared dead, Regrets)
- Mythology/odd setups (Island of 100 Amazons)
- Non-"What If" titles (How I Became an Astronaut)
- Compilations (new, but tiny at start)

## 3. Title rules (from the hits)
- Always starts **"What If You Could See…"** or **"What If [Famous] …You"** — 2nd person "You".
- One concrete noun that's emotionally loaded: *Lie Count, Crush, Body Count, Time Left, Loves You Most.*
- 40–60 characters, no clickbait words, no caps.
- "Count / How long / How much / Who" = implies a **number or ranking** → instant curiosity.

## 4. Thumbnail rules (all hits share this)
- Bright 2D cartoon, purple/blue night background, high saturation.
- Mascot: round **white blob-head guy in a yellow hoodie** — always reacting (shock, smirk, crying).
- **2-word label top-left**, white + yellow: "LIES COUNTER", "BODY COUNT", "DEATH VISION", "LOVES YOU MOST".
- **A glowing number** on screen ("9,317 +1", "3s / 3 YEARS", "1 vs 90", "2 HOURS").
- An attractive female character in most hits (romance tension).
- Flop thumbnails (Regret, Declared Dead, MrBeast) have the same style → **topic, not thumbnail, decides the ceiling.**

## 5. Script / story structure (from chapters + descriptions)
1. **0:00 Cold open with the rule in one line**: "Above every face… a red number… only you can see it." Then an instant personal shock: "Your mom's is one. Your dad's is forty-seven."
2. **Give the hero a job/setting that maximises the power**: new judge (lies), high-school cafeteria (crush), best man at wedding (loves most).
3. **Chapters every ~60–70 sec** (10–14 chapters per 10-min video) — each chapter = a new scene/re-hook ("Day one", "The mirror", "Saturday dinner", "The ring").
4. **Escalate to someone close**: parents, best friend, girlfriend, bride.
5. **Moral dilemma climax** + emotional ending (often quiet/bittersweet).
6. **Description ends with a dilemma question** ("Do you ask the questions, or keep the life you had?") → comments.
7. **Real science sources** listed (psych papers, CDC, NASA) + "What-if parts are our own extrapolation" — xkcd-style credibility.
8. Narration is 2nd person ("you") throughout.

## 6. Packaging/length
- Sweet spot **9–11 min** (best: 10:10). Early 3–5 min science explainers capped at ~25–29K.
- Daily upload rhythm; strongest topics get 100K+ within 4–11 days.
- Tags barely used — titles + thumbnails do all the work.

## 7. Start-to-end playbook for your Kip channel (don't copy titles 1:1 — same engine, own topics)

**Step 1 — Pick topic** using the test: *Is it love, lies, sex, death, money, or betrayal? Is there a NUMBER or RANKING above heads? Would a 16–25-year-old want to know this about their crush/friends/parents?* If no → skip.

**Step 2 — Title**: "What If You Could See ___?" / "What If [Anime character] Trained You for 1 Year?"

**Step 3 — Thumbnail**: Kip reacting + 2-word label + glowing number + one other character (crush/friend/parent).

**Step 4 — Script**: 1-line rule → shocking personal number in first 20 sec → setting that maximises the power → 10–14 one-minute chapters → betrayal by someone close → dilemma ending → comment question.

**Step 5 — Description**: 2-line setup, chapters, sources, dilemma question.

**Step 6 — Cadence**: 4–7 videos/week, 9–11 min each. Double down on whatever hits 50K in 3 days.
